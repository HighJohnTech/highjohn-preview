Four lenses

Revenue. Reliability. Risk. Security.

Revenue

  • Calls, forms, lead response, routing, booking, follow-up, quotes, attribution, and customer handoffs.

Reliability

  • Failure paths, duplicate handling, weak integrations, manual dependencies, missing alerts, and recovery gaps.

Risk

  • Vendor dependence, unclear ownership, single points of failure, fragile processes, and unreviewed automation.

Security

  • Identity, permissions, secrets, data access, AI-agent tools, infrastructure, and integration boundaries.
What happens during the session

We map how the business actually works.

We walk through the customer journey, workflows, people, systems, data movement, existing automation, and the places where work depends on memory or manual handoffs. The goal is diagnosis — not forcing you into a predetermined product.

Revenue review

Observable customer-side failures can be reviewed without turning the session into a security test.

Where appropriate, we can examine public or customer-facing paths such as forms, response time, booking, follow-up, routing, and handoffs. This is not penetration testing and does not include unauthorized active testing.

Authorized technical scope

Deeper AI or systems red-team testing is separately scoped.

If the Discovery Call identifies a need for active technical testing, HJT defines the systems, authorization, boundaries, data handling, test cases, and stop conditions before that work begins.

What you receive

A prioritized decision document — not a pile of tool recommendations.

  • Confirmed findings and evidence available from the engagement
  • Revenue, reliability, risk, and security issues ranked by importance
  • What should be remediated before new technology is added
  • What is worth automating or rebuilding
  • What can stay exactly as it is
  • A recommended next step with scope boundaries
